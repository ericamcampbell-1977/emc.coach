Questions people ask

What's the difference between coaching and therapy?

Coaching is forward-focused and action-oriented: we work on goals, decisions, and growth. Therapy addresses mental health and healing. If something comes up that's better suited to a therapist, I'll tell you and help you find support.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality is a core part of the ICF Code of Ethics. The limits of confidentiality, including for sponsored engagements paid for by an employer, are spelled out in our coaching agreement before we begin.

Can my company pay for coaching?

Often, yes. Many organizations fund leadership coaching from professional development budgets. I'm happy to talk with your sponsor and set up a three-way agreement.

Where do sessions happen?

Sessions are held over video, so you can join from anywhere. In-person sessions in the Kansas City area are available on request.

Let's see if we're a fit.

A discovery call is 30 minutes, free, and no pressure. You'll leave with at least one useful idea either way.
