Birth Chart Reading

A 30-minute, $75 written reading built on the Hellenistic tradition of astrology. The chart is calculated from your exact birth details with astronomical software, then read step by step so each layer builds on the last. It arrives as a PDF by email.

How your chart is read

  1. SectWhether you were born in daytime or at night. This decides which planets work more easily for you and which have to work harder.
  2. ConditionHow strong or strained each planet is by sign (domicile, exaltation, detriment, fall), and its relationship to the Sun, including whether it is hidden in the Sun’s glare.
  3. AspectsWhich planets see each other, and which are in aversion: unable to see each other at all.
  4. HousesWhole-sign houses, the system the early sources used and the one their house meanings were written for.
  5. LotsThe Lot of Fortune (body, circumstances) and the Lot of Spirit (mind, purpose), worked out differently for day and night births.
  6. TimingAnnual profections and zodiacal releasing set the periods of your life. Current planetary transits are read inside that picture, not on their own.

Your birth time must be exact, to the minute. The rising sign and every house depend on it. Check your birth certificate or hospital record. If you only have an estimate, please do not order this reading yet.
