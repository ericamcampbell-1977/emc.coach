Self-Assessment — Scored Instrument

Differentiation of Self

Seventy-eight scored items across eight domains, plus a validity check and an impact check. Answer from a specific, real, recent instance in each section — not from who you'd like to be.

Before you begin: if you answer from self-image rather than the instance you recall, your score will describe your self-concept, not your actual pattern. There's no penalty for a low score here except a wasted exercise.
This is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical assessment. Your answers are scored in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to email your results from your own email app.
