The Archetypal Pattern Inventory

A twelve-archetype instrument in the tradition of Jung and Carol Pearson's Awakening the Heroes Within. It doesn't sort you into one type — it maps which archetypal patterns are most active in you right now, across the three stages of the individuation journey.

This is a well-person instrument. It measures the salience of ordinary human patterns — not disorder, deficiency, or fixed identity. Archetypes shift across a lifetime; a low score simply means that pattern is quieter in you today. Answer for how you actually are, not how you'd like to be.

Psychometric appendix

This instrument follows the structure of the Pearson-Marr Archetype Indicator: twelve archetype subscales, six items each, clustered developmentally into Ego (Innocent, Orphan, Warrior, Caregiver), Soul (Seeker, Lover, Destroyer, Creator), and Self (Ruler, Magician, Sage, Jester). Items are original (clean-room) and mapped to each archetype's core motivation. They are administered round-robin so the factor structure stays blind during response, with balanced keying — three forward and three reverse items per subscale, so uniform high responding resolves to the 3.50 scale midpoint on every archetype rather than inflating any score.

Scoring is ipsative-leaning: because archetype salience is relative, your ranking carries more signal than any single absolute score. Each subscale mean (1–6) is banded within your own profile — Dominant, Active, Latent, or Dormant.
